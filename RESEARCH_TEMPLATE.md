# QFAIM Research Report Template

## Research scope
Date/time and time zone; benchmark; actual universe; source of constituent list; market-cap definition; inclusion/exclusion and liquidity rules.

## Company scores

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Do not assign numerical scores until the underlying data and calculations exist. State exclusions and effective weight redistribution.

## Company research
For each: business model, valuation, earnings and balance-sheet drivers, catalysts, downside cases, macro exposure, management guidance versus analyst consensus, evidence quality.

## Conclusion
Ranked or provisional shortlist, benchmark context, sensitivity analysis, major open questions, next research actions.
